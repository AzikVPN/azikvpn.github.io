Azik VPN Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 24, 2026

Azik VPN ("we", "the app") respects your privacy. This policy explains what data the app collects, how it is used and who it is shared with.

1. No account required

Azik VPN does not require registration. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts or photos.

2. VPN traffic

When you connect, your internet traffic is encrypted and sent through the VPN server of the country you choose. The app does not record the websites you visit, the content of your traffic or your DNS queries. Our VPN servers do not store logs of your browsing activity.

3. Data stored on your device

The server list from our subscription, your settings (language, theme, chosen server) and the remaining free time are stored only on your device. The server list is stored encrypted.

4. Analytics (Google Firebase)

To understand how the app is used and to improve it, we use Google Firebase Analytics. It collects anonymous usage data: app opens, connection attempts and results, the country of the chosen server, device model, Android version, app version and approximate region (derived by Google from the IP address). It does not include server addresses, keys or visited websites. This data is processed by Google LLC under the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Notifications (Firebase Cloud Messaging)

We use Firebase Cloud Messaging to send news and update notifications. For this, Firebase assigns your device an anonymous token. You can turn notifications off in your phone settings at any time.

6. VPN sharing

If you enable VPN sharing, other devices on your hotspot or Wi-Fi can use your VPN connection through a local proxy. Only devices on your local network can connect. The app does not collect data about these devices.

7. Sharing of data

We do not sell your data and do not show ads. Data is shared only with Google (Firebase and Google Play) as described above.

8. Data retention and deletion

Data on your device is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data in the phone settings. Analytics data is kept by Google according to the Google Analytics retention settings and is then deleted automatically.

9. Children

Azik VPN is not intended for children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
